Privacy & support

Your data in Donna

Published by Donna Tasks & Notes. Policy date: October 15, 2026.

Donna keeps your Tasks, Notes and Saved Dictation on your devices. It uses your Apple Account’s iCloud services to sync personal content and share Offices. Members of an Office can read that Office’s shared content according to their access. Your personal Labels, reminders, Saved Dictation and personal preferences are not shared with Office members.

Dictation is processed on your device. Donna does not save or upload raw audio. After a Task is saved, Donna does not keep a separate copy of what you dictated. Unfinished or failed entries remain recoverable until you save or discard them. Words dictated into a Note become Note content. Existing Saved Dictation stays until you delete it; deleting it keeps its Tasks. Optional saved edits are a separate local recovery feature, available for up to 30 days and subject to the item’s deletion and current Office access. They do not provide general version history or cloud backup.

Donna’s Calendar shows dated Tasks inside Donna. It does not connect to an outside calendar. Reminders, Office activity notifications and widgets may show Task information under your device’s settings. You can change notification access and previews in system settings.

Export Data in Settings lets you save a portable JSON copy of durable personal and accessible shared data available on this device, including Saved Dictation. Changes not yet synced here may be missing. Temporary saved edits and unfinished drafts are not included. Donna cannot restore these files. An export is a separate copy outside Donna’s protection, and your chosen file provider may process or sync it. Keep it somewhere safe. Copies already exported or shared cannot be withdrawn by removing Office access.

You can edit your content, move items to Trash, restore them from Trash, delete retained dictation, and permanently delete items where your Office role permits. Removing your own access is different from deleting an Office for its members. Deletion does not remove copies another person has already exported or independent device backups. Donna does not promise a deletion deadline for Apple’s operational backups.

Donna has no advertising/tracking SDK or hosted transcription service. Local diagnostics record technical outcomes without Task/Note text. Apple’s optional device diagnostics follow your system settings. On Mac, local agents are off by default; if you enable them or a Markdown folder, connected software and that folder’s provider have their own data handling.

For help or privacy requests, email wcarlanova@gmail.com. If you email, the support mailbox receives your address and the information you choose to send. Avoid private Task/Note contents, full data stores, passwords and audio; a synthetic example is usually sufficient. Support correspondence is separate from Donna’s content store. We keep support messages only while handling your request and necessary follow-up, then delete them unless legally required to keep them.

Get help

For help with Donna or questions about your data, email wcarlanova@gmail.com.

Find your version/build in Settings → About Donna. Include it, your device and operating-system version, what you expected, what happened, and the steps to reproduce it. For sharing problems, include the Office role, network state and approximate time. Use a synthetic example and remove private information from screenshots. Do not send passwords, encryption keys, full Donna data stores or raw audio.

If a change does not save or sync, keep Donna and its saved data intact. Record the visible status. Do not uninstall the only copy of unsynced work, reset storage or create replacement copies as a troubleshooting step. Sync normally runs automatically when Apple services and the network are available.

Settings → Export Data saves a portable copy of authorized local content. Changes not yet synced here may be missing, and Donna cannot restore the exported file. Keep independent copies of information you cannot afford to lose. Saved Dictation can be deleted separately without deleting its Tasks.
